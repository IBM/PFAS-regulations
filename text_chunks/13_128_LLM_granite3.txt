
SUMMARY
Prohibits  person  from  selling  or  distributing  foodware  containers  containing  perfluoroalkyl  or polyfluoroalkyl substances.
Prohibitions  become  operative  January  1,  2025.  Establishes  civil  penalties  for  violations.
Directs Department of Environmental Quality to establish certification program for compostable products.
