"PFAS chemical" means any of the perfluoroalkyl substances or polyfluoroalkyl substances included in the United States Environmental Protection Agency's expanded ToxCast chemical inventory.
"PFAS manufacturer" means a person or entity who manufacturers or assembles a product containing PFAS chemicals or whose brand name is affixed to a product containing PFAS chemicals. If the product is imported into the United States and the person or entity who manufacturers or assembles the product or whose brand name is affixed to the product does not have a presence in the United States, the importer or first domestic distributor of the product is a "PFAS manufacturer".
"Product" means an item that is manufactured, assembled, or otherwise prepared for sale or distribution to consumers and that is sold or distributed for personal, residential, commercial, or industrial use, such as for use in making another item.
