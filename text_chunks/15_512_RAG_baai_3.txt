A PFAS manufacturer who sells or distributes a product containing intentionally added PFAS chemicals for use in the State on or before December 31, 2024 shall submit the written notification required under this subsection no later than 30 days before January 1, 2025.
A PFAS manufacturer who sells or distributes a product containing intentionally added PFAS chemicals for use in the State on or after January 1, 2025 shall submit the written notification required under this subsection at least 30 days after the PFAS manufacturer sells or distributes the product.
No later than 30 days after the Agency receives a written notification from a PFAS manufacturer under this subsection, the Agency shall publish the trade name of the product and the name of the PFAS manufacturer of the product on the Agency's website.
If there is a subsequent change in information after being provided in a notification under this subsection, the PFAS manufacturer shall notify the Agency of the change not less than 30 days after the date that the PFAS manufacturer becomes aware of it.
