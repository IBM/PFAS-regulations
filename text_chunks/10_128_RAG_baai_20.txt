(4)  Lead,  mercury,  cadmium,  and  hexavalent  chromium, and  PFAS,  on  the  basis  of available  scientific and medical evidence, are of particular concern;(5) It is desirable as a first step in reducing the toxicity of packaging waste to eliminate the addition  of these heavy metals and PFAS to packaging; and
