DIGEST
Proposed  law  defines  the  following  terms:  Class  B  fire  fighting  foam,  person,  PFAS chemicals, and testing.
Proposed law prohibits the discharge of Class B fire fighting foam that contains intentionally added PFAS chemicals except under one of the following conditions:
(1) The discharge or use is during prior prevention or in response to an emergency fire fighting operation.
(2) The discharge or use is during training at a facility with containment, treatment, and disposal measures that prevent uncontrolled release of the Class B fire fighting foam into the environment.
