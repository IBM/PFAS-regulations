
CURRENT VERSION OF TEXT
'Perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances' or 'PFAS' means substances that include any member of the class of fluorinated organic chemicals containing at least one fully fluorinated carbon atom.
STATEMENT
This  bill  would  prohibit,  beginning  two  years  after  the  bill's effective  date,  the  sale,  manufacture,  distribution,  and  use  of  any class  B  firefighting  foam  containing  intentionally  added  PFAS within  the  State.    As  defined  in  the  bill,  'perfluoroalkyl  and polyfluoroalkyl substances' or 'PFAS'  means  substances  that include  any  member  of  the  class  of  fluorinated  organic  chemicalsThe bill's  provisions  would  not  apply  to  the  sale,  manufacture, 6 distribution,  or  use  of  class  B  firefighting  foam  for  which  the 7 inclusion of PFAS  is required by federal law or regulation, 8 including,  but  not  limited  to,  14  C.F.R.  s.139.317.    If  a  federal 9 requirement  to include PFAS  in  class  B  firefighting foam  is 10 revoked,  the  bill's provisions  would  apply  one  year  after  the 11 requirement is revoked. 12
