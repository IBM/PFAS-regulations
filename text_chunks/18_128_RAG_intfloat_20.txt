AN  ACT  Relating  to  the  use  of  toxic  chemicals  in  cosmetic 1 products;  amending  RCW  43.21B.110  and  43.21B.300;  adding  a  new 2 chapter to Title 70A RCW; and prescribing penalties. 3
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON: 4NEW  SECTION. Sec.  1. (1)  The  legislature  finds  that  certain 5 chemicals used in cosmetic products are linked to harmful impacts on 6 health,  such  as  cancer,  birth  defects,  damage  to  the  reproductive 7 system,  organ  system  toxicity,  and  endocrine  disruption.  Many  of 8 these  chemicals  have  been  identified  by  the  state  of  Washington  as 9 high priority chemicals of concern. 10(2)  In  order  to  ensure  the  safety  of  cosmetic  products  and 11 protect  Washington  residents  from  toxic  exposures,  the  legislature 12 intends  to  prohibit  use  of  toxic  chemicals  found  in  cosmetic  and 13 personal  care  products  and  join  other  jurisdictions  in  creating  a 14 safer global standard for cosmetic products and bringing more 15 sustainable, safer ingredients to the marketplace. 16
